# Code of conduct

FairyWriter's public spaces should be useful, curious, and safe for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Discuss ideas and behavior, not a contributor's worth.
- Be specific, patient, and open to correction.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use
  sexualized attention.
- Respect project scope and maintainers' responsibility for technical and
  community decisions.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. The
maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome;
abuse is not.
